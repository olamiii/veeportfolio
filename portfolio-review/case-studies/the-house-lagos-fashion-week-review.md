## 1. Executive verdict

This case study shows the most strategic thinking of the four in its contribution list: identifying a shared audience, aligning the opportunity with Pulse's creators platform, and extending the relationship into an experience. Those are the right moves for a partnerships role. It is held back because the reader never learns what The House is, what the closing-party experience involved, or what came of it. Fix first: one sentence on The House, a clear description of the experience, and what each party gained.

## 3. What works

1. **Strategic contribution list**: shared audience, platform alignment and experience extension are exactly the skills a partnerships role needs.
2. **Clear personal role**: "Developed the collaboration".
3. **Real documentation**: three Instagram links show the work happened.

## 4. What isn't working

### Severity: High: The House is never explained
- **Evidence:** "The House" appears only as a partner name.
- **Why it matters:** a reader outside Lagos cannot judge the fit or the value of the collaboration.
- **Fix:** one sentence on what The House is and why it fit.

### Severity: High: The experience is described abstractly
- **Evidence:** "an experiential opportunity around the Lagos Fashion Week closing party."
- **Why it matters:** the most interesting part of the work cannot be pictured.
- **Fix:** describe the experience concretely: what it was, who took part, what creators did.

### Severity: High: No outcome beyond "it happened"
- **Evidence:** the Execution section has one line.
- **Why it matters:** no result to discuss in an interview.
- **Fix:** add content produced, creator involvement, reach or partner feedback, with sources.

### Severity: Medium: Abstract language throughout
- **Evidence:** "the intersection of fashion, media and community" (homepage card)
- **Why it matters:** generic phrasing hides specific judgment.
- **Fix:** replace with concrete nouns: who, where, what.

### Severity: Low: No reflection
- **Evidence:** not present.
- **Why it matters:** misses a chance to show growth.
- **Fix:** one line.
