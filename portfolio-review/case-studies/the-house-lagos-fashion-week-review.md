**First impression:** Now concrete: two named events, a venue, a drinks partner and an attendance figure. It still reads as an event listing rather than a partnership with a goal and a result. Previous score: 38.

## 1. Executive verdict

This moved from vague to specific. A reader now knows the two events (The Fashion Insider Dinner with Kisara, and The House x Pulse Fashion Week Closing Party), that Macallan was the drinks partner, and that Pulse Creator Collective attended with 100+ creators, designers and fashion enthusiasts. The gap is purpose: there is no stated goal, so the attendance figure has nothing to be measured against, and it is unclear which event it covers. Fix first: add a one-line goal for Pulse Creator Collective and say whether 100+ covers both events.

## 4. What isn't working

- **Fix:** add one line on what Pulse wanted.

### Severity: Medium: Attendance scope unclear
- **Evidence:** "100+ creators, designers and fashion enthusiasts"
- **Why it matters:** an interviewer will ask.
- **Fix:** "across both events" or name the event.

### Severity: Medium: No reflection

### Severity: Low: One visual
