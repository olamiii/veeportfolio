# Case Study Review: ART X Lagos x Pulse

**Project:** ART X Lagos x Pulse media partnership, Pulse
**Source:** `partnerships/art-x-lagos.html` (rendered locally, commit `bebbc42`) and its homepage card
**Target Level:** Not specified, reviewed against Senior (read as a senior brand and partnerships role; the rubric is built for product design, so Interaction Design is excluded)
**Review Mode:** Online Portfolio
**Overall Score:** 36/100 (32 of 90 applicable points; Interaction Design N/A)
**Evidence Coverage:** Medium
**Hiring Readiness:** Needs Significant Work

**Could not inspect:** the linked Instagram reel. The page shows a still frame from it, which was inspected.

**First impression:** A reader learns that Oluwatoni secured a media partnership between Pulse and ART X Lagos with an editorial angle; they do not learn what the partnership included, what was published or what either side gained.

## 1. Executive verdict

The case study states a clear, credible personal role ("Developed and secured the partnership") and a genuinely strategic angle: editorial storytelling about the people behind African art rather than event coverage. That angle is the best thing here and deserves more space. What holds it back is that the deal itself is invisible: no terms, no deliverables, no published stories and no reason ART X Lagos said yes. Fix first: describe the proposition you took to ART X Lagos and what the partnership actually produced, keeping proposed access separate from delivered content.

## 3. What works

1. **A clear personal role**: securing a partnership is a concrete, ownable achievement.
2. **A strategic angle**: going beyond event coverage to the people behind the art shows editorial and commercial judgment.
3. **Honest outcome framing**: the page does not claim interviews or access that may not have happened.

## 4. What isn't working

### Severity: High: The deal and its deliverables are invisible
- **Evidence:** "Media partnership secured between Pulse and ART X Lagos." (The outcome)
- **Why it matters:** a hiring manager for a partnerships role wants to see what you negotiated, not only that a partnership exists.
- **Fix:** add what Pulse offered, what ART X Lagos offered, and what was delivered.

### Severity: High: No published content or result
- **Evidence:** no links or screenshots of stories produced.
- **Why it matters:** the editorial angle cannot be judged without the editorial output.
- **Fix:** link and screenshot the content that ran; label proposed items as proposed.

### Severity: Medium: Problem and objective are generic
- **Evidence:** "For Pulse, the opportunity went beyond covering an art event."
- **Why it matters:** without a goal the result has nothing to be measured against.
- **Fix:** state Pulse's goal in one sentence.

### Severity: Medium: Bullets instead of a story
- **Evidence:** five bullets under My contribution.
- **Why it matters:** tasks read as activity, not judgment.
- **Fix:** turn the bullets into a short sequence: opportunity, proposition, negotiation, result.

### Severity: Low: No reflection
- **Evidence:** not present.
- **Why it matters:** misses a chance to show growth.
- **Fix:** one specific lesson.

## 5. Storytelling audit

- **Opening hook:** good subtitle, but it describes the partnership rather than what changed.
- **Narrative structure:** Problem and Insight are thin, Decision is buried in a bullet, Outcome stops at "secured".
- **Clarity:** high.
- **Pacing:** too short to carry a decision.
- **Decision rationale:** missing.
- **Outcome presentation:** honest but thin.

**Remove:** the "What it shows" line.
**Move:** the editorial angle into the title and opening.
**Condense:** the five contribution bullets into two sentences.
**Expand:** the proposition and what was produced.

## 6. Visual evidence audit

- **Supports the story:** the reel still shows Pulse present at ART X Lagos.
- **Missing:** a proposal or deck slide, published stories, partner credit placements.
- **Needs annotation or redoing:** the reel still is a small, low-resolution frame (203 x 360 source).
- **Interactions to demonstrate:** none.
- **Understandable from images and captions alone?** No: the image shows presence, not the partnership.

## 7. Evidence and credibility audit

## 10. Hiring manager questions

1. What did Pulse offer ART X Lagos, and what did ART X Lagos offer Pulse?
2. Why editorial storytelling instead of standard coverage, and who needed convincing?
3. What content was published as a result, and how did it perform?
4. Were any proposed interviews or access opportunities not delivered, and why?
5. Who else at Pulse was involved, and what did you own?
6. Is the relationship continuing?
