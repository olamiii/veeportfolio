**Project:** ART X Lagos x Pulse, media partnership
**Source:** `partnerships/art-x-lagos.html` (second review, deployed commit) and its homepage card
**Target Level:** Not specified, reviewed against Senior (read as a senior brand and partnerships role; the rubric is built for product design, so Interaction Design is excluded)
**Review Mode:** Online Portfolio
**Overall Score:** 48/100 (43 of 90 applicable points; Interaction Design N/A)
**Evidence Coverage:** Medium
**Hiring Readiness:** Needs Significant Work

**Could not inspect:** the linked Instagram interview. The page's still frame was inspected.

**First impression:** The goal and the named interviewees now make this credible at a glance; what is missing is what the content did once it was published. Previous score: 36.

## 1. Executive verdict

The biggest change is that the result is now specific and confirmed: exclusive content with Tokini Peterside, Founder of ART X, and Dr Jareh Das, curator of the ART X Icon section, plus other gallerists and curators. That fixes the first review's main credibility issue, where proposed access read as if it might not have happened. The goal is clear too. What holds it back is that the story stops at "content was made": there is no reach, no audience response, no sense of what ART X gained, and no reflection. Fix first: add one line on how the content performed and one on what ART X got.
