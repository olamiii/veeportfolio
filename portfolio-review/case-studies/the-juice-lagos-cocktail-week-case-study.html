The idea: a booth people play, not pass

The central idea was simple: take a digital lifestyle community beyond the screen and into a shared cultural experience.

We built the booth around The Juice's voice, with signs reading "Main Squeeze", "Take A Sip" and "Certified Juice Head", and a prize wheel at its centre. Visitors could spin for a Shout-Out, a Feature Post, Hot Seat, Free Juice, a sticker or a hand fan, or spin again. Several of the rewards only make sense on Instagram, so the game pulled people from the booth onto The Juice's page.
