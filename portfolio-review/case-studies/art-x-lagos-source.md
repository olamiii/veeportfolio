ORIGINAL BRIEF FROM OLUWATONI (2026 partnerships)
PROJECT 1: ART X LAGOS x PULSE
Category: Cultural Partnerships | Media | Editorial Storytelling
Developed and secured a media partnership between Pulse and ART X Lagos, connecting Pulse's media and storytelling capabilities with one of Africa's leading contemporary art platforms.
The collaboration created opportunities to explore the people, ideas and creative voices shaping the African art ecosystem through editorial storytelling.
My contribution included: developing the partnership opportunity and alignment between both platforms; working on the partnership proposition and stakeholder engagement; exploring editorial opportunities that would create value beyond conventional event coverage; prioritising access to influential cultural voices and deeper storytelling opportunities; working towards creating a stronger connection between the ART X Lagos experience and Pulse's audience.
The case study should demonstrate my ability to identify cultural partnership opportunities and connect media platforms with influential creative ecosystems.
Do not imply that every proposed interview or access opportunity was delivered unless confirmed.
