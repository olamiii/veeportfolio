The experience
A booth people played, not passed.
The centrepiece was a spin-the-wheel game. Attendees spun the wheel for a chance to win prizes: free mocktails at our stand, a feature post on The Juice, and other fun extras.
Every spin was a chance to introduce The Juice, talk to people and invite them to subscribe to the newsletter.
The goal
Bring The Juice's digital community into an existing community of Lagos lifestyle, cocktail and nightlife lovers.
Introduce the brand to the Cocktail Village audience, let them interact with us, and get them to subscribe to the newsletter.
My contribution
Led the partnership activation between The Juice and Lagos Cocktail Week.
Worked across planning, brand experience and execution of The Juice's presence at Cocktail Village.

The result
100+ new newsletter subscribers.
Instagram grew from ~300 to 565 followers during the activation period, +88.3%.
What it shows
Translating a digital brand into a physical experience its audience wants to be part of.

ORIGINAL BRIEF FROM OLUWATONI (2026 partnerships)
PROJECT 2: THE JUICE x LAGOS COCKTAIL WEEK
Category: Experiential Partnerships | Brand Activation | Audience Engagement
Led the partnership activation between The Juice, Pulse's lifestyle and pop culture newsletter, and Lagos Cocktail Week.
The collaboration brought The Juice's digital community into the physical Cocktail Village through an interactive branded booth experience.
Results: The Juice's social media community grew from approximately 300 followers to 565 followers during the campaign period, representing approximately 88.3% growth. (Later clarified by Oluwatoni: these are Instagram figures, growth during the activation period.)
Do not attribute all follower growth exclusively to the partnership without qualification.
My contribution involved leading the partnership activation and working across the planning, brand experience and execution of The Juice's presence at Cocktail Village.
Central message: "Taking a digital lifestyle community beyond the screen and into a shared cultural experience."
Instagram reel: https://www.instagram.com/reels/De1WVp2IsPk/

LATER FACTS FROM OLUWATONI (22 November 2026)
The spin-the-wheel was an interactive game at the event: attendees spun the wheel and stood a chance to win free prizes, like free mocktails at our stand, a feature post on The Juice platform, and other fun stuff.
The goal of the partnership for Pulse was to bring our digital community into an existing community of lovers of Lagos lifestyle, cocktails and nightlife. The Juice is a pop culture newsletter and we saw an opportunity to plug into the audience at the Cocktail Village, introduce the brand, let them interact with us and subscribe to the newsletter.
We gained 100+ subscribers.
Instagram figures: ~300 to 565 followers, +88.3%, during the activation period.
