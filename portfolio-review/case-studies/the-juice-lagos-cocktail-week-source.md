ORIGINAL BRIEF FROM OLUWATONI (2026 partnerships)
PROJECT 2: THE JUICE x LAGOS COCKTAIL WEEK
Category: Experiential Partnerships | Brand Activation | Audience Engagement
Led the partnership activation between The Juice, Pulse's lifestyle and pop culture newsletter, and Lagos Cocktail Week.
The collaboration brought The Juice's digital community into the physical Cocktail Village through an interactive branded booth experience.
Results: The Juice's social media community grew from approximately 300 followers to 565 followers during the campaign period, representing approximately 88.3% growth. (Later clarified by Oluwatoni: these are Instagram figures, growth during the activation period.)
Do not attribute all follower growth exclusively to the partnership without qualification.
My contribution involved leading the partnership activation and working across the planning, brand experience and execution of The Juice's presence at Cocktail Village.
Central message: "Taking a digital lifestyle community beyond the screen and into a shared cultural experience."
