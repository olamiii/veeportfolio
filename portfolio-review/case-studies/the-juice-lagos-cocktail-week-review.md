## 1. Executive verdict

This is now the strongest of the four. The goal is explicit ("introduce the brand ... and get them to subscribe to the newsletter"), the booth mechanic is shown and explained with your photo, and the outcomes map straight onto the goal: 100+ newsletter subscribers and honestly qualified Instagram growth. What keeps it just under Promising is what any senior partnerships reviewer will ask next: what Lagos Cocktail Week got from the deal, when the activation ran and how the numbers were measured, and what you learned. Fix first: add partner value and a two-line reflection.

## 3. What works

1. **Goal and result match**: the case states a subscriber goal and reports a subscriber result.
2. **A concrete, memorable mechanic**: the prize wheel is easy to picture and clearly yours.
3. **Honest numbers**: the Instagram figure is qualified as running alongside regular content.

## 4. What isn't working

### Severity: High: Partner value is missing
- **Evidence:** the page covers Pulse's goal only.

- **Fix:** add what Lagos Cocktail Week gained.

### Severity: Medium: No dates or measurement source
- **Evidence:** "during the activation period"

### Severity: Medium: No reflection

### Severity: Low: Decision rationale is partial

### Severity: Low: "What it shows" filler
