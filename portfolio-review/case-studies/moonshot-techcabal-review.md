**Source:** `partnerships/moonshot-techcabal.html` (rendered locally, commit `bebbc42`) and its homepage card
**Target Level:** Not specified, reviewed against Senior (read as a senior brand and partnerships role; the rubric is built for product design, so Interaction Design is excluded)

**Could not inspect:** the linked Instagram reel. The page shows a still frame from it, which was inspected.

**First impression:** A reader learns that Pulse partnered with Moonshot by TechCabal and that Oluwatoni "worked on" it; they cannot tell what she did, what the partnership involved, or what came of it, so it reads as the weakest of the four.

## 1. Executive verdict

The page names a relevant, credible partner and a sensible strategic reason (putting Pulse inside Africa's tech and business conversations). Almost everything else a hiring manager needs is missing: a personal contribution, what the partnership included, and any result beyond a link to a reel. As it stands it slightly weakens the set, because the vague role invites the question of how involved you were. Fix first: state exactly what you owned and what the partnership delivered; if you cannot say much publicly, shorten this into a smaller "also worked on" entry rather than a full case study.
