# Case Study Review: Moonshot by TechCabal x Pulse

**Project:** Moonshot by TechCabal x Pulse, media partnership
**Source:** `partnerships/moonshot-techcabal.html` (second review, deployed commit) and its homepage card
**Target Level:** Not specified, reviewed against Senior (read as a senior brand and partnerships role; the rubric is built for product design, so Interaction Design is excluded)
**Review Mode:** Online Portfolio
**Overall Score:** 28/100 (25 of 90 applicable points; Interaction Design N/A)
**Evidence Coverage:** Low
**Hiring Readiness:** Not Ready

**Could not inspect:** the linked Instagram post. The page's still frame was inspected.

**First impression:** The role is now clear and personal, but a reader still cannot tell what the partnership involved or what it produced. Previous score: 22.

## 1. Executive verdict

The improvement is ownership: "Coordinated the partnership from conception to execution" and "Made sure every agreed deliverable was delivered" tell a reader exactly what you did. But the deliverables themselves are never named, so "All agreed deliverables delivered" cannot be judged. There is no goal beyond "a route into those industry conversations", and no outcome beyond presence. This is the case a reviewer will skip. Fix first: list the deliverables and say what Pulse got from them.
