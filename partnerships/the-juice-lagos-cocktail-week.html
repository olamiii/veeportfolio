The Juice × Lagos Cocktail Week

Taking a digital lifestyle community beyond the screen and into a shared cultural experience.

PartnersThe Juice (Pulse) · Lagos Cocktail Week
FormatInteractive branded booth, Cocktail Village
Year2026
My roleLed the partnership activation
~300 → 565followers in The Juice's social community
+88.3%growth during the campaign period

Measured across the whole campaign period. The activation was a central part of it, alongside The Juice's regular content.

The context

  • The Juice is Pulse's lifestyle and pop culture newsletter, with a community built online.
  • Lagos Cocktail Week's Cocktail Village offered a cultural moment where that community could meet the brand in person.

My contribution

  • Led the partnership activation between The Juice and Lagos Cocktail Week
  • Worked across planning, brand experience and execution
  • Shaped The Juice's presence inside Cocktail Village

Execution & outcome

  • An interactive branded booth at Cocktail Village
  • Social community grew from ~300 to 565 followers during the campaign, +88.3%

What it shows

Translating a digital brand into a physical experience its audience wants to be part of.
