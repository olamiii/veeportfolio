The context

  • The Juice is Pulse's lifestyle and pop culture newsletter, with a community built online.
  • Lagos Cocktail Week's Cocktail Village offered a cultural moment where that community could meet the brand in person.

My contribution

  • Led the partnership activation between The Juice and Lagos Cocktail Week.
  • Worked across planning, brand experience and execution.
  • Shaped The Juice's presence inside Cocktail Village.

Execution & outcome

  • An interactive branded booth at Cocktail Village.
  • Instagram grew from ~300 to 565 followers during the activation period, +88.3%.

What it shows

Translating a digital brand into a physical experience its audience wants to be part of.
